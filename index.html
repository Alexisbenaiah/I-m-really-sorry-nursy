I'm Truly Sorry

I don't know if the word "sorry" is enough to heal the hurt I caused.

Sometimes a few words spoken in a moment can leave a wound that lasts much longer than we ever intended.

Of all the things I regret, the one that hurts the most is knowing that I was the reason for your pain.

I never wanted to make you feel unappreciated, disrespected, or hurt. Yet somehow, through my own words, I did exactly that.

If I could go back to that moment, I would choose my words more carefully. I would choose kindness. I would choose understanding. I would choose anything that wouldn't leave you carrying the weight of what I said.

The truth is, I haven't stopped thinking about it. Not because I'm afraid of losing you, but because I can't ignore the fact that someone so special to me was hurt because of my actions.

What makes this even harder for me is that I never wanted our story to be remembered because of a mistake. I wanted the chance to know you better. To learn about the little things that make you smile, the dreams you quietly carry, the challenges you've overcome, and the experiences that made you who you are today. I wanted to know the person behind the smile, behind the strength, and behind the kindness that I've come to admire. And that's why I regret what I said. Because instead of bringing us closer to understanding each other, my words may have created distance between us.

The truth is, I wasn't hoping for something perfect. I was simply hoping for the opportunity to continue getting to know you—your thoughts, your stories, your perspective, and the things that matter most to you. Little by little. One conversation at a time.

Some people come and go without leaving much behind. But there are people whose value you truly realize the moment you risk losing them. You are one of those people.

I know forgiveness cannot be demanded, and I have no right to expect it. But I hope that one day, when the pain becomes lighter, you'll remember that my regret is real, and that I genuinely wish I had done better.
There is something I want you to know.

I am not hurting because I made a mistake.

I am hurting because the person I hurt was you.

What makes it even more painful is knowing that I may have hurt someone I genuinely wanted to know better. Someone whose story I wanted to hear. Someone whose thoughts I wanted to understand. Someone whose presence I appreciated more than I ever expressed.

I wasn't asking for perfection. I wasn't expecting anything extraordinary. I was simply grateful for the chance to know you a little more with each conversation.

And if life gives me another opportunity, I hope it won't be remembered as the person who hurt you— but as someone who learned from his mistake and became better because of it.

❤️ Because the truth is, I still hope for the chance to know the amazing person you are, beyond this mistake.
— From someone who genuinely regrets hurting you.
